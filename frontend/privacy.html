Privacy Policy for English Breakfast

Last Updated: September 2026

English Breakfast ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile and web application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in via Google or register with email, we receive your basic profile information (such as your name, email address, and avatar) to manage your account and synchronize your learning progress.
  • Learning Progress & Activity: We store your learning statistics, studied words, quiz results, and weekly XP scores to track your personal achievements and show your position on the weekly leaderboard.
  • Audio / Microphone Data: When you use voice pronunciation exercises, audio is processed locally or securely evaluated to score pronunciation. We do NOT record or store your private conversations.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, personalize, and improve language learning exercises.
  • To synchronize your vocabulary progress across your devices.
  • To maintain weekly league leaderboards.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is protected using industry-standard security protocols and hosted on secure Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

In accordance with Google Play developer policies and privacy regulations, you have full control over your data:

  • You can delete your account and all associated learning history at any time directly in the application under Settings → Account Management → Delete Account & Data.
  • Alternatively, you can request account deletion by contacting us via email.
