   All Portuguese entries restored with Brazilian nuances (e.g. troco, ruim, burro, roupas, cumeada).

2. Polysemy & False Friend Bugs:
   - ID 2006 (change): Russian gloss "изменять, сдача" resulted in "сдача" (change / coins) being machine-translated as "surrender/capitulation" in 16 languages (cs: kapitulace, sk: odovzdanie sa, hu: megadás, el: υποταγή, sl: predaja, et: alistumine, lt: pasidavimas, da: overgive, fi: antautua, sv: ge upp, hr: predati se, lv: padoties, vi: đầu hàng, th: ยอมจำนน, ko: 항복하다, ja: 降伏する, kk: берілу). Calibrated to proper change/currency terms across all languages.
   - ID 2016 (finger): Japanese had "指す" (sasu, verb "to point") instead of the noun "指" (yubi, finger). Calibrated.
   - ID 2033 (stupid): Russian gloss "тупой по жизни, дебил" caused "тупой" (blunt/dull) to be mistranslated as "boring in life" in Nordic and Asian languages (da: kedelig i livet, fi: tylsä elämässä, sv: tråkig i livet, hr: dosadan u životu, lv: garlaicīgs dzīvē, ja: 人生がつまらない, ko: 인생이 지루한, th: น่าเบื่อในชีวิต). Calibrated to natural insults/adjectives for foolish/stupid.
   - ID 2034 (clothes): Confused with "cloth" (fabric/rags) in 11 languages (it: stoffa, pt: pano, ro: pânză, bg: плат, cs: tkanina, sk: látka, hu: szövet, el: πανί, sl: krpo, et: riie, lt: audinys). Calibrated to garments/clothes.
   - ID 2049 (by): Russian gloss "кем, чем, на, ко времени" caused "чем" (instrumental of "что") to be mistranslated as the conjunction "than" (cs: než, sk: než, hu: mint, bg: отколкото, ro: decât, da: end, fi: kuin, sv: än, th: กว่า, kk: қарағанда). Calibrated to proper prepositions.
   - ID 2051 (watch): In 18 languages, only the noun wristwatch was translated, omitting the verb to watch/observe. Calibrated to include both verb and noun meanings.
