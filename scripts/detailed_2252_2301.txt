EXECUTIVE SUMMARY OF MAJOR CRITICAL BUGS DETECTED AND RESOLVED:
1. CAPITAL EXECUTION BUG (ID 2270 - "accomplish"):
   Machine translation translated "accomplish / execute" as CAPITAL PUNISHMENT / EXECUTION OF PRISONERS:
   - Estonian: "hukkama" (execute by death penalty) -> fixed to "saavutama, korda saatma, teostama"
   - Finnish: "teloitus" (execution/death sentence) -> fixed to "saavuttaa, suorittaa, toteuttaa"
   - Vietnamese: "hành quyết" (put to death / execution) -> fixed to "hoàn thành, đạt được"

2. INVESTMENT PROSPECTUS BUG (ID 2290 - "avenue"):
   Russian "проспект" (wide street / avenue, but also advertising prospectus/pamphlet) caused a translation cascade:
   - Finnish: "esite" (brochure / pamphlet) -> fixed to "puistokatu, valtakatu, valtaväylä"
   - Japanese: "目論見書" (financial prospectus) -> fixed to "大通り、並木道、街路"
   - Kazakh: "перспектива" (perspective) -> fixed to "даңғыл, кең көше"
   - Korean: "안내서" (brochure / guidebook) -> fixed to "대로, 가로수길, 길"
   - Vietnamese: "bản cáo bạch" (legal prospectus) -> fixed to "đại lộ, đường lớn"
   - Thai: "เอกสารชี้แจง" (prospectus document) -> fixed to "ถนนสายใหญ่, อเวนิว, ช่องทาง"
   - Swedish/Danish/Croatian: "prospekt" (advertising brochure) -> fixed to "aveny/allé/avenija"

3. POLITICAL REGIME IN POWER BUG (ID 2301 - "mode"):
   Russian "режим" (mode / regime of operation, but also totalitarian/political regime) caused:
   - Japanese: "政権" (political regime in power) -> fixed to "モード、様式、方式"
   - Korean: "정권" (political administration / regime) -> fixed to "모드, 방식, 양식"
   - Finnish: "hallinto" (government administration) -> fixed to "tila, toimintatila, moodi"
   - Danish/Swedish: "regime / regim" (political regime) -> fixed to "tilstand, modus, måde / läge, mod, tillstånd"
   - Thai: "ระบอบการปกครอง" (regime of government) -> fixed to "โหมด, รูปแบบ, วิธีการ"
   - Indonesian: "rezim" (political regime) -> fixed to "mode, cara, ragam"

4. TONE OF VOICE / SOUND VOLUME CASCADE (ID 2285 - "shout at"):
   Over 20 languages had "raise tone / pitch" or "turn up sound volume":
   - Thai: "เพิ่มระดับเสียง" (increase audio volume)
   - Finnish: "nostaa sävyä" (raise tone/shade)
   - Kazakh: "тонды көтеріңіз" (raise tone imperative)
   - Japanese: "口調を上げる" (raise voice pitch)
   - Fixed across all 35 languages to authentic verbal shouting/yelling collocations (кричать на, anschreien, gritarle a, crier sur, huutaa jollekin, 怒鳴りつける, etc.).

5. PROSTITUTION MISTRANSLATION (ID 2283 - "bitch"):
   Arabic ("عاهرة"), Persian ("فاحشه"), Turkish ("orospu"), Hungarian ("kurva"), and Estonian ("lits") translated "bitch" as prostitute/whore.
   Corrected to accurate dual biological (female dog: כלبة, سگ ماده, dişi köpek, nőstény kutya, emakoer) and slang/derogatory woman meanings.

6. PROVINCE ABBREVIATION CONFUSION (ID 2273 - "dna"):
   Italian had "Provincia" (abbreviation collision) -> corrected to "DNA".
   Kazakh had invalid lowercase "дНҚ" -> corrected to "ДНҚ".
   Romanian had article attached "ADN-ul" -> corrected to "ADN".

7. SCHOOL GRADES & DEGREES MISTRANSLATION (ID 2297 - "evaluation"):
   Russian "оценка" (school grade vs evaluation) caused 10 languages to translate "evaluation" as school grade/degree:
   - Greek "βαθμός", Romanian "grad", Italian "grado", Hungarian "fokozat", Czech/Slovak "stupeň", Bulgarian "степен", Estonian "hinne", Slovenian "razred".
   - Calibrated to authentic assessment/evaluation terminology (valutazione, evaluare, hodnotenie, értékelés, αξιολόγηση, etc.).

8. PREPOSITIONAL & ADVERBIAL FALSE FRIENDS:
   - ID 2269 ("beside"): Numerous languages had calqued "next to side" ("рядом сбоку", "obok boku", "accanto al lato", "lângă lateral") or confused it with "nearby / in the vicinity" ("i nærheden", "u blizini", "tuvumā", "近くの"). Calibrated to accurate prepositions (рядом с, возле, neben, al lado de, à côté de, vieressä, etc.).
   - ID 2275 ("consist of"): Ukrainian had "перебувати" (reside/stay) -> corrected to "складатися з, містити".
   - ID 2264 ("physician"): Preserved medical doctor distinction and prevented false-friend physicist drift across Romance, Slavic, Germanic languages.
   - ID 2268 ("recipe"): Preserved cooking recipe and prevented confusion with receipt/quittance.
   - ID 2292 ("scholar"): Corrected from purely natural scientist ("tiedemies", "과학자", "科学者") to learned academic / researcher.
