Batch 2602-2651 Linguistic Audit and Calibration Report
====================================================

Key bugs caught and calibrations made:
1. Word 2602 (thread): Polysemy bug! Dump kk only had "тақырып" (forum topic / conversation thread), completely omitting the primary physical meaning "нить, жіп / жіпше" and mechanical "резьба / бұранда". Calibrated across all locales to cover sewing thread and mechanical thread.
2. Word 2603 (fool): Added comprehensive coverage of both nominal ("дурак, глупец") and verbal ("дурачить, одурачивать") senses across all 35 target languages.
3. Word 2604 (characterize): Harmonized verb forms across all languages to denote both descriptive delineation and distinctive feature attribution ("характеризовать, описывать, отличать").
4. Word 2605 (pie): Regional nuances: Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR) calibrated to "torta, empada, pastel"; Latin American Spanish (es-MX) calibrated to "tarta, pastel, empanada"; accurately reflecting local culinary terminology.
5. Word 2606 (legacy): Expanded beyond narrow historical inheritance to include institutional heritage and technological/computing legacy ("наследие, наследство, достояние").
6. Word 2607 (frankly): Calibrated adverbial expressions of sincerity and directness ("откровенно, честно говоря, прямо") ensuring natural conversational flow.
7. Word 2608 (derive): Portuguese dump had narrow "retirar". Calibrated to "derivar, extrair, obter" covering mathematical derivation, lexical derivation, and extraction of benefits/meaning.
8. Word 2609 (dumb): CRITICAL DUMP BUGS! Portuguese had "margem" (margin - corrupted data shift!) and Kazakh had "күңгірт" (dim/dark). Fixed across all locales to properly reflect both physical muteness ("немой, mudo, stumm") and colloquial stupidity ("глупый, тупой, burro").
9. Word 2610 (scope): CRITICAL DUMP BUG! Portuguese had "legislatura" (legislature - data shift error). Fixed to "alcance, escopo, âmbito". Calibrated all locales for breadth of application, extent, and opportunity.
10. Word 2611 (formation): Expanded to cover both generative process ("формирование, образование") and structural/military layout ("строй, боевой порядок, formacja").
11. Word 2612 (hence): Calibrated transitional adverbs denoting consequence and provenance ("следовательно, поэтому, отсюда").
12. Word 2613 (margin): CRITICAL DUMP BUG! Portuguese had "zero" (data shift). Fixed to "margem, borda, lucro". Calibrated page border, safety leeway, and financial profit margin.
13. Word 2614 (legislature): Fixed part-of-speech and governmental terminology across all languages ("законодательный орган, легислатура, парламент").
14. Word 2615 (sustain): Broadened beyond physical holding to include suffering injuries/losses ("поддерживать, выдерживать, переносить, нести").
15. Word 2616 (miracle): CRITICAL DUMP BUG! Portuguese had "máscara" (mask - data shift). Fixed to "milagre, prodígio, maravilha".
16. Word 2617 (zero): Ensured full numeral, noun, and adjective coverage ("ноль, нуль, нулевой") with proper orthography across Asian and Middle Eastern scripts.
17. Word 2618 (tactic): Calibrated tactical planning, maneuvering, and strategic stratagem ("тактика, тактический ход, маневр").
18. Word 2619 (mask): Polysemy coverage including face covering, medical respirator/mask, disguise, and concealment verb ("маска, личина, маскировать").
