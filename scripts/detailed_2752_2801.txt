Batch 2752-2801 Linguistic Audit and Calibration Report
====================================================

Key bugs caught and calibrations made:
1. Word 2755 (stroke): Catastrophic machine translation hallucination in dump! Across more than 20 languages (fr: fer, it: ferro, ro: fier, bg: желязо, cs/sk/sl: železo, da/sv: jern, fi: rauta, hr: željezo, lv: dzelzs, et: raud, lt: geležis, ar: حديد, vi: sắt, th: เหล็ก, ko: 철, ja: 鉄, kk: темір), dump had translated the metal "iron", and Russian had only "гладить". Calibrated to complete multi-sense coverage: medical cerebrovascular accident (инсульт, Schlaganfall, derrame cerebral, ictus, AVC), physical hit/action (удар, coup), artistic stroke (мазок, штрих, trait, trazo), and gentle petting (поглаживание).
2. Word 2754 (fighting): Part-of-speech mismatch. Dump had infinitives/verbs like Swedish "slåss", Indonesian "bertarung", Japanese "戦う", Thai "ต่อสู้". Calibrated to proper noun/action/participle forms (борьба, бои; kamp, strid; lucha, combate; lutte; 戦い, 戦闘; pertempuran).
3. Word 2757 (duck): Homonym omission. Dump only translated the waterfowl bird (утка, pato, canard, Ente), completely omitting the rapid dodging verb (пригнуться, увернуться, agacharse, esquivar, se baisser). Calibrated to cover both bird and dodge action.
4. Word 2759 (fabric): Critical false friend protection. Fabric != Russian/Slavic "фабрика" (factory/mill), but textile material (ткань, полотно, materija, Stoff, tela, tissu) and structural framework (the fabric of society / структура, Gefüge).
5. Word 2761 (greatly): Dump had cumbersome French phrase "de manière significative" and narrow translations. Calibrated to natural, expressive adverbs: grandement, considérablement, beaucoup, sangat, очень, значительно.
6. Word 2765 (rifle): Polysemy expansion. Added both firearm (винтовка, карабин, fusil, Gewehr, karabin) and verb (обшаривать, рыться, fouiller, rebuscar).
7. Word 2766 (closet): Regional calibration and idioms. Distinguishes wardrobe/cupboard (шкаф, гардероб, armario, ropero, placard) and LatAm Mexican "clóset", as well as metaphorical secret storage.
8. Word 2768 (patch): Polysemy expansion. Dump had partial nouns; calibrated across fabric patch (заплатка, remendo, parche), medical bandage (пластырь, pansement, Pflaster, curita), plot of land (участок, клочок), and software bugfix (патч, patch).
9. Word 2769 (touchdown): Domain polysemy. Added aerospace landing (приземление, посадка, aterrizaje, atterrissage, Landung) and American football scoring (тачдаун, touchdown, touché).
10. Word 2773 (attribute): Homograph and POS calibration. Covers both noun attribute/trait (свойство, признак, trait, atributo, Eigenschaft) and verb to attribute/ascribe (приписывать, относить, attribuer, atribuir, zuschreiben).
11. Word 2775 (virtue): Severe mistranslation in dump! French had "dignité" (dignity) and Arabic had "كرامة" (dignity/pride). Calibrated to genuine virtue: vertu, فضيلة, добродетель, достоинство, Tugend, virtude.
12. Word 2777 (athletic): POS error in dump! Spanish had noun plural "deportes" (sports). Calibrated to proper adjective: atlético, deportivo, musculoso.
13. Word 2780 (facilitate): Machine translation error in dump! French had "contribuer" (contribute) and Arabic had "تعزيز" (strengthen/boost). Calibrated to actual meaning: faciliter, favoriser, rendre aisé; ييسر, يسهل; способствовать, облегчать.
14. Word 2782 (heritage): Fixed narrow dump translation ("наследство" = personal inheritance). Added cultural/historical heritage: наследие, достояние, patrimonio, Kulturerbe, patrimoine, dziedzictwo.
15. Word 2783 (summit): Polysemy fix. Brazilian pt had only "cume" (peak), missing political summit. Calibrated to include cúpula, conferência de cúpula, саммит, meeting of heads of state.
16. Word 2786 (blade): Essential polysemy. Expanded from knife edge to turbine rotor blade (лопасть, pale, aspa, Flügel) and botany (былинка, травинка, brin d'herbe, brizna).
17. Word 2788 (enforce): Overly verbose machine phrases in dump (fr: "garantir la conformité", pt: "garantir a conformidade"). Replaced with authentic legal verbs: faire appliquer, faire respecter; fazer cumprir, aplicar, impor.
18. Word 2791 (concerning): Dual grammar role. Covers preposition (касательно, относительно, regarding, concernant, bezüglich) and adjective (вызывающий беспокойство, тревожный, worrying, préoccupant).
19. Word 2797 (grave): Major homonym fix! Dump had only noun (могила, tumba, tombe, Grab, cova). Calibrated to include crucial adjective: серьезный, мрачный, веский, grave, serio, schwerwiegend.
20. Word 2798 (transaction): Mistranslation in dump! French had "accord" (agreement). Calibrated to transaction, opération, démarche.
21. Word 2800 (consistently): Hilarious machine translation error in dump! French had "séquentiellement" (sequentially). Calibrated to avec constance, régulièrement, systématiquement, invariablement.
22. Regional calibration for pt (pt-BR) and es (es-MX / Latin America):
    - Brazilian Portuguese: gramado (lawn), varejo (retail), cúpula (summit), AVC / derrame cerebral (stroke), esportivo (athletic).
    - Latin American / Mexican Spanish: clóset (closet), curita (patch), pasto (lawn), menudeo (retail).
23. Natural grammar, orthography, and diacritics validated across all non-Latin scripts (hi, ar, fa, ur, bn, kk, ja, zh, ko, th).
