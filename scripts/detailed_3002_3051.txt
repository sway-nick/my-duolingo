Batch 3002-3051 Linguistic Audit and Calibration Report
====================================================

Key bugs caught and calibrations made:
1. Word 3002 (omit): CATASTROPHIC BUG CAUGHT! The dump translated "omit" via Russian "пропускать" as "miss", which cascaded into hilarious errors: Greek "δεσποινίδα" (unmarried woman / Miss!), Estonian "igatsema" (yearn/miss someone!), Czech "miss" (literal English word!), Slovak "minúť" (miss a target!), Slovenian "zgrešiti" (miss a target / sin!), French "manquer", Italian "mancare", Romanian "rata" (miss a target), Kazakh "секіру" (jump!). Calibrated across all 35 target languages to genuine omission/exclusion ("пропускать, опускать, исключать, упускать; auslassen, omettre, omitir, pomijać, παραλείπω, বাদ দেওয়া, حذف کردن").
2. Word 3003 (pit): Homonym & polysemy bug! Dump had only hole/cavity ("fosa, poço, Grube, fosse, λάκκος, گودال, گڑھا"). Completely omitted the stone/seed of stone fruits (peach pit, cherry pit: "косточка (плода), Stein, hueso de fruta, noyau de fruit, pestka, caroço, sâmbure, pecka, kôstka, mag, κουκούτσι, koščica, luuvilja kivi, kauliukas, frugtsten, siemenkivi, stenkärna, koštica, kauliņš, गुठली, نواة, هسته, گٹھلی, hạt, เมล็ด, 씨앗, 種, 果核, дәнек"), mine shafts, orchestra pit, and motorsport pit lane. Both core senses fully calibrated.
3. Word 3004 (suspend): Dump focused narrowly on pause ("приостановить"). Added physical hanging ("подвешивать, натягивать, pendurar, colgar, aufhängen, accrocher") and disciplinary debarment/exclusion from school/work ("відсторонювати, beurlauben, afastar temporariamente, inhabilitar, 정직시키다, 停职").
4. Word 3007 (consult): Calibrated dual active usage: seeking advice from a specialist ("проконсультироваться, советоваться, pedir consejo, rådføre sig, 相談する") and consulting/referring to reference material ("обращаться к словарю/справочнику, nachschlagen, se référer à, ανατρέχω σε, 参照する").
5. Word 3008 (expertise): HORRIFIC FALSE FRIEND / HOMOPHONE BUG! Japanese dump had "検査" (inspection/testing!) and Korean had "검사" (inspection/medical exam!). German had only "Sachverstand". 6. Word 3010 (provided): Conjunction vs verb inflection mismatch! Dump had past tense 3rd person singular verb ("предоставил", "proporcionó", "قدّم", "প্রদান করেছে", "ارائه کرد") ignoring that "provided (that)" is overwhelmingly used as a conditional conjunction ("при условии что, если; sofern, vorausgesetzt dass, always provided that, pourvu que, siempre que, a condizione che, pod warunkiem że, بشرط أن, به شرط اینکه, बशर्ते कि, 只要") as well as passive participle ("предоставленный, bereitgestellt, fourni, provisto"). Both calibrated cleanly.
