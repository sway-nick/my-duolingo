Batch 3202-3251 Linguistic Audit and Calibration Report
====================================================

Key bugs caught and calibrations made:

1. Word 3243 (skull): CATASTROPHIC HOMOPHONE BUG!
   Dump conflated "skull" (cranium / bony head framework) with "scull" (rowing boat oar / pair of oars) across 6 languages:
   - fr: "godille" (scull oar) -> calibrated to "crâne, boîte crânienne"
   - ro: "vâsle" (oars) -> calibrated to "craniu, țeastă"
   - cs: "krátké veslo" (short oar) -> calibrated to "lebka, lební klenba"
   - hu: "evezőlapát" (oar blade) -> calibrated to "koponya"
   - el: "κωπή" (rowing) -> calibrated to "κρανίο, νεκροκεφαλή"
   - lt: "irklas" (oar) -> calibrated to "kaukolė"

2. Word 3244 (pursuit): TRIPLE BUG (SHIFT, PERSECUTION CONFLATION, AND ENCODING CORRUPTION)!
   a) Copy shift: French had "crâne" (skull) carried over from previous line -> calibrated to "poursuite, quête, recherche".
   b) Conflation with "persecution" (religious/ethnic oppression):
      - vi: "bức hại" -> calibrated to "sự theo đuổi, sự truy đuổi, mưu cầu"
      - th: "การข่มเหง" -> calibrated to "การไล่ตาม, การติดตาม, การมุ่งมั่นหา"
      - ko: "박해" -> calibrated to "추구, 추적, 탐구"
      - ja: "迫害" -> calibrated to "追求, 追跡, 探求"
      - et: "tagakiusamine" -> calibrated to "jälitamine, püüdlus, tagaajamine"
      - fi: "vaino" -> calibrated to "takaa-ajo, tavoittelu, pyrkimys"
   c) Raw corrupted string in Arabic: dump contained "سعي، 追 pursuit، ملاحقة" -> sanitized to clean "ملاحقة, مطاردة, سعي".

3. Word 3232 (automatic): CATASTROPHIC AUTOMOBILE CONFLATION!
   Dump erroneously translated "automatic" as AUTOMOBILE / MOTOR VEHICLE across 10 languages:
   - it: "auto" -> "automatico, involontario"
   - pt: "auto" -> "automático, maquinal"
   - ro: "auto" -> "automat, spontan"
   - cs: "auto" -> "automatický, samovolný"
   - sk: "auto" -> "automatický, samovoľný"
   - hu: "autó" -> "automatikus, önműködő"
   - sl: "avto" -> "avtomatski, samodejen"
   - et: "auto" -> "automaatne, isetoimiv"
   - lt: "automobilis" -> "automatinis, savaiminis"
   - bg: "автоматично" (adverb) -> "автоматичен, машинен"

4. Word 3230 (premise): CATASTROPHIC LIVING ROOM FALSE FRIEND!
   Dump translated singular "premise" as an individual ROOM in an apartment across 11 languages:
   - da: "rum", fi: "huone", sv: "rum", hr: "soba", lv: "istaba", vi: "phòng", th: "ห้อง", ko: "방", ja: "部屋", kk: "бөлме", ru/uk: "помещение / приміщення".
   In English, singular "premise" in argumentation/logic is an assumption, thesis, hypothesis, or proposition ("предпосылка, исходное положение"); only in the plural ("premises") does it denote real estate or business grounds, and NEVER an individual living room. Calibrated accurately across all 35 languages.

5. Word 3226 (loop): BICYCLING CONFLATION BUG!
   Dump translated "loop" as "bicycling / cycling" across 10 languages through a mechanical machine translation chain (loop -> cycle -> bicycling):
   - tr: "bisiklet sürme" -> "döngü, ilmik, halka"
   - ro: "ciclism" -> "buclă, laț, circuit"
   - bg: "колоездене" -> "примка, петля, цикъл"
   - cs: "cyklistika" -> "smyčka, oblouk, cyklus"
   - sk: "cyklistika" -> "slučka, okruh, cyklus"
   - hu: "kerékpározás" -> "hurok, zárt lánc, ciklus"
   - el: "ποδηλασία" -> "βρόχος, θηλιά, κύκλωμα"
   - sl: "kolesarjenje" -> "zanka, obroč, ponavljalni cikel"
   - et: "jalgrattasõit" -> "silmus, aas, tsükkel"
   - lt: "važiavimas dviračiu" -> "kilpa, ciklas, žiedas"

6. Word 3235 (credibility): SYSTEMIC SEMANTIC DISTORTION (CREDIBILITY vs POWER/AUTHORITY)!
   Dump translated "credibility" as political/state authority or power ("власть", "autorità", "autoritate", "hatóság", "εξουσία", "quyền lực", "อำนาจ", "권위", "権威", "билік") across 20+ locales.
   Credibility is believability, trustworthiness, plausibility, and credence.
   Calibrated to: "достоверность, надежность, убедительность", "Glaubwürdigkeit", "credibilidad", "crédibilité", "wiarygodność", "αξιοπιστία", "verodostojnost", "troværdighed", "uskottavuus", "senimdilik".

7. Word 3221 (parade): CATASTROPHIC COPY BUG!
   Dump had Portuguese `"pt": "informação"` (copied from word 3218 "info").
   Calibrated to "desfile, parada, desfilar".

8. Word 3222 (archive): CATASTROPHIC COPY BUG!
   Dump had Spanish `"es": "desfilar"` (copied from word 3221 "parade").
   Calibrated to "archivo, archivar".

9. Word 3211 (deploy): MASSIVE CORRUPTION & POS MISMATCHES!
   - it: "download" (!) -> calibrated to "schierare, dispiegare, implementare"
   - hu: "telepítés" (noun) -> "bevet, állomásoztat, telepít"
   - bg: "разположи" (aorist past) -> "разгръщам, разполагам, внедрявам"
   - fi: "käyttöönotto" (noun) -> "ottaa käyttöön, sijoittaa, ryhmittää"
   - hr: "raspoređivanje" (verbal noun) -> "rasporediti, razmjestiti, razviti"

10. Word 3227 (altogether): NEGATIVE POLARITY & GENERALIZATION BUGS!
    - Negative polarity: pt ("de forma alguma"), ro ("deloc"), it ("affatto"), el ("καθόλου") meant "not at all".
    - Conflation with "generally": da ("generelt"), fi ("yleisesti"), sv ("i allmänhet"), hr ("općenito"), vi ("nói chung"), th ("โดยทั่วไป"), ko ("일반적으로"), ja ("一般的に").
    Calibrated to "completely, totally, all in all, in total" ("в целом, всего, полностью, совсем", "insgesamt, völlig", "en total, por completo").

11. Word 3245 (therapist): HEALTHCARE FALSE FRIEND!
    In Russian, Ukrainian, and Kazakh medicine, "терапевт" is a primary care internist / general practitioner (GP), whereas English "therapist" denotes a psychotherapist, physical therapist, or mental health counselor.
    Calibrated to include "психотерапевт, терапевт, специалист по реабилитации".

12. Word 3229 (crush): DUAL-SENSE COMPREHENSIVE ENRICHMENT!
    - sl ("simpatija") and et ("armumine") only covered romantic crushes;
    - most Western languages only covered mechanical crushing/flattening.
    Calibrated dual senses (physical compression/destruction AND romantic infatuation) across all languages.
