Batch 3252-3301 Linguistic Audit and Calibration Report
====================================================

Key bugs caught and calibrations made:
1. Word 3252 (execution): Dual-sense polysemy calibrated! Dump had one-sided translations in et, fi, da, sv, hr, ar, vi, th, ko, ja (only death penalty / capital punishment) vs ru, uk, de, cs (only task implementation). Calibrated both essential senses across all 35 target languages: 1) execution of a plan, command, program, or legal sentence ("исполнение, выполнение, Ausführung, réalisation, cumplimiento"); 2) capital punishment ("казнь, Hinrichtung, mise à mort, poprava, إعدام, 처형, 処刑, 处决").
2. Word 3253 (pal): Fixed Romanian vocative form in dump ("prietene" -> "prieten, amic, tovarăș"). Calibrated colloquial friendship terms across all locales (de: Kumpel/Freund/Kamerad, es: amigo/compañero/colega, pt: amigo/companheiro/parceiro, fr: pote/copain/ami, ur: دوست، یار، ساتھی).
3. Word 3254 (chuckle): Part-of-speech and tonal precision: dump had generic laughter verbs ("lachen" DE, "rire" FR, "cười" VI, "γέλιο" EL noun). Calibrated to quiet, suppressed chuckle, chortle, or giggle as both verb and noun ("хихикать, посмеиваться; смешок", "kichern, schmunzeln", "reír entre dientes; risita", "glousser; petit rire", "chichotać; chichot", "cluck/giggle" nuances).
4. Word 3257 (kinda): CRITICAL SYSTEMIC BUG CAUGHT! Dump erroneously translated "kinda" (colloquial contraction of "kind of" = somewhat, rather, a bit, in a way) as "as if / though" across 7+ locales (Arabic كما لو, Hindi मानो, Japanese まるで, Korean 마치 ~처럼, Romanian ca și cum, Vietnamese như thể, Thai ราวกับว่า). Calibrated to genuine "rather / somewhat / kind of" across all locales ("вроде, типа, как бы, отчасти", "irgendwie, sozusagen, ein bisschen", "algo, un poco, más o menos", "un peu, en quelque sorte, plutôt", "نوعاً ما، إلى حد ما", "थोड़ा, कुछ हद तक", "약간, 다소, 어느 정도", "ちょっと、ある程度").
5. Word 3258 (relieve): CATASTROPHIC MACHINE TRANSLATION BUG CAUGHT! In Portuguese dump had "torne isso mais fácil" (imperative sentence "make this easier"!), in Romanian "fă-o mai ușoară" (imperative sentence "make it easier"!), in Greek "κάνω το πιο εύκολο" (sentence "I make the easiest"!), and in French "faciliter les choses" (phrase). Calibrated to genuine verbs "облегчать, успокаивать, сменять", "aliviar, mitigar, relevar", "soulager, apaiser, relayer", "lindern, erleichtern, entlasten, ablösen", "a alina, a ușura, a schimba", "ανακουφίζω, καταπραΰνω, απαλλάσσω", covering both easing pain/burden and relieving on duty.
6. Word 3260 (corridor): Improved Spanish from ambiguous "corredor" (which often means runner or financial broker) to standard "pasillo, corredor"; calibrated hallway/passageway and transit corridor senses across all locales.
7. Word 3263 (pad): Polysemy bug! Dump included graphic software overlay layer ("suprapunere" RO, "қабаттастыру" KK, "επικάλυψη" EL, "lớp phủ" VI, "重ね合わせ" JA, "ओवरले" HI). Calibrated to real-world senses: writing notepad, protective cushion/guard, ink/launch pad ("блокнот, подушечка, накладка, щиток", "Notizblock, Polster, Kissen", "bloc de notas, almohadilla", "bloc-notes, tampon").
8. Word 3264 (tuck): CATASTROPHIC "REFUEL" BUG CAUGHT! "tuck" was translated as "REFUEL / REFUELING" across 4 distinct locales! (Greek "εφοδιάζω πάλι με καύσιμα", Romanian "realimentare", Kazakh "жанармай құю", Portuguese "reabastecer"). Corrected to tucking in clothes/bed/folding edges ("заправлять, подтыкать, прятать, засовывать", "meter, doblar, remeter", "rentrer, border, replier", "hineinstecken, einstecken", "enfiar, dobrar, ajeitar", "a băga înăuntru, a împături", "διπλώνω, μαζεύω, χώνω", "қыстыру, тығу, бүгу").
9. Word 3265 (railroad): Fixed adjective forms in Greek ("σιδηροδρομικός") and Romanian ("feroviar") to proper nouns "σιδηρόδρομος", "cale ferată".
10. Word 3266 (minimize): RIDICULOUS BUG CAUGHT! Portuguese dump had "travesseiro" (pillow!). Calibrated to "minimizar, reduzir ao mínimo" across all locales.
11. Word 3269 (nonsense): Fixed Spanish plural "disparates" to singular nouns "tontería, disparate, sinsentido"; fixed Romanian "Prostii" capitalization and plurality.
12. Word 3270 (leap): Fixed Portuguese "quicar" (which means bounce like a ball!) to "salto, pulo; saltar, pular". Calibrated jump/spring action as verb and noun.
13. Word 3271 (rider): Polysemy bug! German had "Fahrer" (driver!), Spanish "jinete" (horseman only), Portuguese "cavaleiro" (horseman only), Vietnamese "người cưỡi ngựa" (horse rider only). Calibrated to multi-faceted passenger, equestrian, cyclist, and legal/contract rider clause across all 35 locales ("всадник, наездник, пассажир, райдер", "Reiter, Mitfahrer, Beifahrer, Zusatzklausel", "jinete, pasajero, ciclista, cláusula adicional").
14. Word 3273 (terrific): Prevented false-friend conflation with terrifying/frightening in Romance languages (es: magnífico/estupendo, fr: formidable/sensationnel, it: magnifico/straordinario, pt: sensacional/fantástico).
15. Word 3275 (oversee): MAJOR BUG CAUGHT! German had "beobachten" (watch/observe!), Portuguese "observar", Arabic "يراقب", Romanian "observa", Greek "παρατηρώ", Vietnamese "quan sát", Japanese "観察する", Korean "관찰하다". All translated "oversee" as passive observation rather than executive supervision! Calibrated to supervise/manage/direct ("курировать, надзирать, beaufsichtigen/leiten, supervisar, superviser/chapeauter, يشرف على، يدير").
16. Word 3278 (suburban): BIZARRE BUGS CAUGHT! Kazakh dump had "жұмысқа баратын адам" (a commuter!), Greek had "μικροαστικός" (petite-bourgeois!). Calibrated to suburban/residential outskirts ("пригородный, vorstädtisch, suburbano, de banlieue, қала маңындағы, προαστιακός").
17. Word 3279 (momentum): CATASTROPHIC "PULSE" BUG CAUGHT! Romanian dump had "puls", Greek had "σφυγμός", Portuguese had "pulso" (all meaning heart pulse/wrist!). Arabic had "دافع" (motive), Japanese had "衝動" (impulse/urge), Korean had "충동" (impulse/urge). Calibrated to genuine physics and figurative momentum / impetus / forward drive ("импульс, инерция, Schwung, ímpeto/embalo, élan/dynamique, avânt/elan, ορμή/ώθηση, زخم/قوة دافعة, 탄력/추진력").
18. Word 3281 (clerk): Turkish had archaic Ottoman "katip". Calibrated to modern administrative, retail sales, and court clerks across all locales ("клерк, служащий, конторщик, продавец", "Büroangestellter, Sachbearbeiter", "oficinista, empleado, dependiente", "funcionario, balconista, atendente").
19. Word 3284 (proposition): Fixed verb infinitive bugs in Portuguese ("oferecer") and Romanian ("oferi"). Calibrated to formal proposition, assertion/thesis in logic, and business proposal ("предложение, тезис, суждение", "proposición, propuesta", "Vorschlag, Behauptung, Proposition").
20. Word 3286 (beam): STRUCTURAL LOG/TRUNK BUG CAUGHT! Dump had "log / raw tree trunk" (бревно, tronco, kłoda, buștean, kütük, ท่อนซุง, khúc gỗ, бөрене) instead of shaped structural beam / girder / rafter / joist ("балка, брус, viga, Balken, poutre, grindă, δοκός, арқалық"). Balanced with light beam / laser beam.
21. Word 3288 (verse): Fixed whole poem conflation (de: Gedicht, ro: poem, tr: şiir, ja: 詩). Calibrated to specific poetic line, stanza, or biblical verse ("стих, строфа, куплет, Vers, Strophe, verso, estrofe").
22. Word 3289 (casual): MASSIVE "DAILY" BUG CAUGHT! German had "täglich" (daily!), Greek "καθημερινός", Romanian "în fiecare zi" (every day!), Thai "ทุกวัน" (every day!), Vietnamese "hàng ngày" (daily!), Indonesian "sehari-hari" (daily!), Kazakh "күнделікті" (daily!). Casual means relaxed, informal, nonchalant, or accidental! Calibrated across all locales ("повседневный, неформальный, непринужденный", "lässig, zwanglos, leger", "informal, desenfadado, casual", "décontracté, informel").
23. Word 3291 (shove): Russian dump had narrow slang "засунуть" (shove it!). Calibrated to primary physical shove / thrust / push roughly ("толкать, пихать, совать; толчок", "schubsen, stoßen", "empujar, empellón").
24. Word 3292 (carbohydrate): Fixed French plural "glucides" -> singular "glucide, hydrate de carbone".
25. Word 3293 (steam): Fixed Romanian imperative "gătiți cu abur" and Turkish verb-only "buharla pişirmek". Restored noun water steam + verb to steam cook ("пар, водяной пар; варить на пару", "Dampf; dämpfen", "vapor; cocinar al vapor", "vapeur; cuire à la vapeur").
26. Word 3296 (resume): DUAL HOMOGRAPH FIX! Resumé (CV / summary) vs. resume (restart / continue after pause). Dump had only summary in Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, only CV in French, and only verb in Turkish. Calibrated dual coverage across all 35 languages ("возобновлять, продолжать; резюме", "fortsetzen, wieder aufnehmen; Lebenslauf", "reanudar, continuar; currículum", "reprendre; CV", "retomar, recomeçar; currículo").
27. Word 3297 (interior): Calibrated noun (inside, indoor space, domestic affairs) and adjective (inner, internal) across all locales ("интерьер, внутренность; внутренний", "Innenraum; innenliegend", "interior; interno").
28. Word 3298 (full-time): CRITICAL "PERMANENT BASIS" BUG CAUGHT! Russian had "на постоянной основе", Portuguese "de forma contínua", Arabic "بشكل دائم", Hindi "स्थायी आधार पर", Japanese "恒久的に", Korean "영구적으로", Turkish "sürekli olarak", Romanian "în mod continuu", Greek "σε συνεχή βάση", Thai "อย่างถาวร", Kazakh "тұрақты негізде". ALL mistranslated full-time (working full hours / standard weekly workload) as permanent! Corrected to full working hours / full-time ("полный рабочий день, на полную ставку", "Vollzeit, vollzeitlich", "a tiempo completo", "à temps plein", "período integral", "cu normă întreagă", "دوام كامل", "पूर्णकालिक", "フルタイム", "전일제").
29. Word 3299 (questionnaire): Enhanced Russian from rare "вопросник" to standard "анкета, опросный лист, вопросник".
30. Word 3300 (behalf): Calibrated prepositional phrases "on behalf of / in behalf of" across all languages ("от имени, ради, в интересах", "im Namen von, zugunsten", "en nombre de, de parte de", "au nom de", "em nome de").
31. Word 3301 (graph): CATASTROPHIC "SCHEDULE/CALENDAR" BUG CAUGHT! French dump had "calendrier" (calendar!), Arabic "جدول زمني" (timetable!), Hindi "अनुसूची" (schedule!), Japanese "スケジュール" (schedule!), Korean "일정" (schedule!), Greek "πρόγραμμα" (program/schedule!), Romanian "programa" (syllabus!), Thai "ตารางเวลา" (timetable!), Vietnamese "lịch trình" (itinerary!). ALL mistranslated "graph" as "schedule"! Calibrated to genuine mathematical graph / chart / plot ("график, диаграмма, граф", "Diagramm, Graph, Schaubild", "graphique, diagramme, graphe", "gráfica, gráfico, diagrama", "رسم بياني، مخطط", "图表、坐标图", "グラフ", "그래프").
