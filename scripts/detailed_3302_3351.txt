Batch 3302-3351 Linguistic Audit and Calibration Report
====================================================

Key bugs caught and calibrations made:
1. Word 3313 (electoral): CATASTROPHIC BUG CAUGHT! Dump erroneously translated electoral as SELECTIVE in 20+ locales (fr: sélectif, it: selettivo, ro: selectiv, bg: селективен, cs: selektivní, sk: selektívny, hu: szelektív, el: εκλεκτικός, sl: selektivno, et: valikuline, lt: selektyvus, da: selektiv, fi: valikoiva, sv: selektiv, hr: selektivni, lv: selektīvs, ar: انتقائي, vi: chọn lọc, th: คัดเลือก, ko: 선택적인, ja: 選択的, kk: селективті)! Calibrated to genuine election-related terminology across all 35 languages (électoral, elettorale, volební, választási, εκλογικός, volilni, valimis-, rinkimų, valg-, vaali-, val-, izborni, vēlēšanu, انتخابي, bầu cử, เกี่ยวกับการเลือกตั้ง, 선거의, 選挙の, 选举的, নির্বাচনী, сайлау).
2. Word 3338 (accommodate): CATASTROPHIC BUG CAUGHT! Dump translated the verb accommodate as a NOUN meaning place/location (fr: lieu, it: posto, ro: loc, bg: място, cs: místo, sk: miesto, hu: hely, el: θέση, sl: kraj, et: koht, lt: vieta, da: sted, fi: paikka, sv: plats, hr: mjesto, lv: vieta, ar: مكان, vi: địa điểm, th: สถานที่, ko: 장소, ja: 場所, kk: орын)! Restored active verbs covering lodging/housing (unterbringen, héberger, loger, alojar, hospedar, pomieścić, ubytovat, elszállásol, majutama, huse, majoittaa) and adapting to needs (przyjąć, dostosować się, vyhovět, вмещать, размещать, приспосабливать, يستضيف، يتسع لـ).
3. Word 3348 (contributor): CATASTROPHIC BUG CAUGHT! Dump translated contributor as participant/attendee (participant, participante, katılımcı, participant, участник, účastník, résztvevő, συμμέτοχος, udeleženec, osaleja, dalyvis, deltager, osallistuja, deltagare, sudionik, dalībnieks, प्रतिभागी, مشارك, peserta, người tham gia, ผู้เข้าร่วม, 참가자, 参加者, қатысушы) across 20+ locales. Restored genuine contributor meanings: article author/collaborator and financial donor/supporter.
4. Word 3318 (serving): SYSTEMIC BUG CAUGHT! In culinary and nutrition contexts, serving means a portion or helping of food. Dump translated it literally as table setting (сервировка стола: fr: mise en place de la table, ro: aranjarea mesei, bg: сервиране на маса, cs: prostírání, sk: prestieranie stola, hu: asztalterítés, el: στρώσιμο τραπεζιού, sl: postavitev mize, et: lauakujundus, lt: stalo serviravimas) or public service (ko: 봉사하는, ja: 奉仕する, kk: қызмет көрсету). Calibrated to food portions and dietary servings (порция, portion, ración, adag, μερίδα, porcija, portsjon, annos, खुराक, وجبة، حصة طعام, پرس غذا, 1인분, 1人前, 一份食物, тағам үлесі).
5. Word 3333 (pickup): PART-OF-SPEECH BUG CAUGHT! Dump translated noun pickup as verb infinitives (подобрать, підібрати, ramasser, toplamak, vyzvednout, felvenni, σηκώνω, korja üles, menjemput, उठाना, алу) or imperatives (вземете, poberi). Calibrated to the noun pickup: pickup truck (пикап, Pritschenwagen, camioneta, picape, kisteherautó, ημιφορτηγό, ladvogn, avolavapakettiauto, kamionet, पिकअप ट्रक, شاحنة صغيرة, وانت, รถกระบะ, 픽업트럭, ピックアップトラック, 皮卡车, пикап көлігі), collection/retrieval of goods/passengers, and acoustic audio transducer.
6. Word 3325 (contend): PART-OF-SPEECH BUG CAUGHT! Dump translated contend as a noun (fight, combat: lutte, combattimento, kavga, luptă, битка, boj, harc, πάλη, võitlus, kova, kamp, taistelu, borba, cīņa, قتال, การต่อสู้, 싸움, төбелес). Calibrated to verb contend in both senses: struggle/compete (бороться, соперничать, kämpfen, contender, lutter, rywalizować, çekişmek) AND argue/assert (contend that..., утверждать, отстаивать, behaupten, verfechten, sostener, soutenir, twierdzić, iddia etmek, afirmar, állít, ισχυρίζομαι, hävda).
7. Word 3304 (herb): BOTANICAL CONFUSION CAUGHT! Dump conflated culinary/medicinal herb with lawn grass (Gras, Grün, grama, çimen, iarbă, γρασίδι, ruoho, græs, gräs, zāle, rumput, cỏ, หญ้า). Calibrated to culinary herbs and medicinal plants (Kraut, Küchenkräuter, hierba aromática, erva, erba aromatica, zioło, билка, gyógynövény, βότανο, zelišče, ravimtaim, vaistažolė, krydderurt, yrtti, ört, ljekovita biljka, ārstniecības augs, जड़ी-बूटी, عشب عطري، عشبة طبية, گیاه دارویی, جڑی بوٹی, rempah herbal, thảo mộc, สมุนไพร, 허브, ハーブ, 香草, ভেষজ, дәрілік шөп).
8. Word 3312 (continuing): PART-OF-SPEECH BUG CAUGHT! Dump had continuation nouns (продолжение, Fortsetzung, continuation, continuazione, continuação, pokračování, jätkamine, fortsættelse, jatkuminen, fortsättning, nastavak, turpinājums, निरंतरता, استمرار, kelanjutan, жалғасы). Calibrated to active adjective/participle (продолжающийся, длящийся, непрерывный; fortlaufend, andauernd; continuo, permanente; continu, persistant; ciągły, nieustanny; devam eden; contínuo; continuu; продължаващ; pokračující; folyamatos; συνεχής; nadaljnji; jätkuv; tęstinis; vedvarende; jatkuva; pågående; kontinuiran; nepārtraukts; निरंतर; مستمر; ادامه‌دار; مسلسل; berkelanjutan; liên tục; ต่อเนื่อง; 지속적인; 継続的な; 持续的; ক্রমাগত; жалғасып жатқан).
9. Word 3319 (safely): ADVERB / ADJECTIVE FIX! Dump used adjectives in da: sikker, fi: turvallinen, sv: säker, lv: drošs, ar: آمن, id: aman, th: ปลอดภัย, ko: 안전한, ja: 安全な. Calibrated to genuine adverbs (sikkert, turvallisesti, säkert, droši, بأمان، بسلام, dengan aman, อย่างปลอดภัย, 안전하게, 安全に, 安全地).
10. Word 3345 (stance): SEMANTIC DRIFT CAUGHT! Dump translated stance as geographical position or office post (tr: konum, ar: منصب, vi: vị trí, th: ตำแหน่ง, ko: 위치, ja: 地位). Fixed to ideological stance/standpoint (Haltung, Standpunkt, postura, attitude, stanowisko, postoj, álláspont, στάση, stališče, seisukoht, ståndpunkt, stajalište, nostāja, रुख, موقف, دیدگاه, مؤقف, sikap, lập trường, ท่าที, 입장, 姿勢, 立场, ұстаным) and physical standing posture/body stance.
11. Word 3347 (dot): Kazakh dump had ұпай (game score points!). Fixed to нүкте, дақ. Korean dump had 지점 (spatial location!). Fixed to 점, 반점.
12. Word 3349 (cooperate): Kazakh dump had noun ынтымақтастық (cooperation). Fixed to verb ынтымақтасу, бірігіп жұмыс істеу. Arabic dump had noun/past تعاون. Fixed to active verb يتعاون، يتكاتف.
13. Word 3351 (offend): Arabic dump had noun إهانة (insult). Fixed to verb يُهين، يجرح المشاعر. Romanian dump had 3rd person ofensează. Fixed to infinitive a jigni, a ofensa.
14. Word 3346 (rabbit): Czech dump had adjective králičí (rabbit's). Fixed to noun králík. Croatian dump had zec (hare). Calibrated to kunić, pitomi zec.
15. Word 3341 (comprise): Italian dump had affermare (to assert!). Fixed to comprendere, consistere in, includere. Bulgarian, Slovak, Slovenian had 3rd person plurals (включват, zahŕňajú, vključujejo). Fixed to proper lemmata.
16. Word 3309 (mechanic): Turkish dump had adjective mekanik (mechanical). Fixed to tamirci, mekaniker, motor ustası.
17. Word 3306 (fossil): Czech, Hungarian, Lithuanian dumps had adjectives (fosilní, fosszilis, iškastinis) omitting noun zkamenělina, kövület, iškasena. Calibrated across both noun and adjective senses.
18. Word 3330 (abstract): Dump omitted polysemy (Greek, Croatian, Hindi, Vietnamese, Thai only translated summary/abstract noun, while others only translated adjective). Restored both abstract thought and paper abstract/summary across all locales.
19. Brazilian Portuguese (pt / pt-BR) adaptations applied (picape, caminhonete, ônibus de traslado, vaivém, azeitona, verde-oliva, acomodar, colaborar, disco rígido).
20. Latin American / Mexican Spanish (es) nuances applied (camioneta, reses, ganado bovino, proyección, furgoneta, disco rígido).
