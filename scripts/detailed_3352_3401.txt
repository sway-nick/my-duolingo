Batch 3352-3401 Linguistic Audit and Calibration Report
====================================================

Key bugs caught and calibrations made:
1. Word 3391 (openly): CATASTROPHIC BUG CAUGHT! Dump erroneously translated the adverb 'openly' as the infinitive/finite VERB 'to open' in 5+ locales (fr: "ouvrir", it: "aprire", ar: "يفتح", ja: "開く", kk: "ашу")! Calibrated to genuine adverbs across all 35 target languages (ouvertement, apertamente, علناً, 公然と/率直に, ашық/жасырмай/бүкпесіз, open, abiertamente, etc.).
2. Word 3393 (fever): CATASTROPHIC SYSTEMIC BUG CAUGHT! In almost all languages in the dump, 'fever' was translated as literal "high temperature" (de: "hohe Temperatur", fr: "haute température", it: "alta temperatura", pl: "wysoka temperatura", pt: "alta temperatura", ar: "درجة حرارة عالية", hi: "उच्च तापमान", ja: "高温", kk: "жоғары температура") rather than standard medical and colloquial nouns! Calibrated to authentic terms: Fieber, fièvre, febbre, gorączka, febre, febră, láz, πυρετός, حمى, تب, بخار, 發燒/发烧, 熱, қызба across all locales.
3. Word 3374 (clause): CATASTROPHIC BUG CAUGHT! Dump translated 'clause' as "dot/bullet point" (ar: "نقطة", hi: "बिंदु", ja: "点", kk: "нүкте") or generic paragraph (fr/it/pt: "paragraphe"/"parágrafo")! Calibrated to contractual and legal clause/stipulation (Klausel, cláusula, clause, clausola, madde, ρήτρα, بند في عقد, ماده قرارداد, 条款/细则) as well as grammatical clause (Teilsatz, proposition subordonnée, zdanie składowe/podrzędne, yan cümle, 从句).
4. Word 3360 (counterpart): CRITICAL REGISTER / SEMANTIC BUG CAUGHT! Dump erroneously reduced 'counterpart' to an ordinary work colleague / desk-mate ("коллега", "Kollege", "collègue", "collega", "kolega z pracy", "zamíl", "सहकर्मी", "同僚", "әріптес"). In diplomatic, corporate, and formal contexts, 'counterpart' refers specifically to the corresponding official holding the equivalent position in another government or organization (Amtskollege, homologue, controparte, contraparte, mevkidaş, omolog, protějšek, náprotivok, ομόλογος, vaste, نظير/مقابل, ہم منصب, người đồng cấp, 对应职位者). Calibrated to accurately reflect both equivalent peer and parallel entity.
5. Word 3353 (compel): PART-OF-SPEECH BUG CAUGHT! Dump translated the transitive verb 'compel' as nouns meaning physical strength/power (ar: "قوة", ja: "力", kk: "күш қолдану"). Calibrated to active transitive verbs: zwingen, obligar, contraindre, costringere, zorlamak, يجبر/يرغم, مجبور کرنا, 強いる/強制する, 强迫/迫使, мәжбүрлеу/зорлау.
6. Word 3371 (passing): CATASTROPHIC BUG CAUGHT! Dump translated the noun/gerund 'passing' as an architectural aisle or physical corridor/walkway (ar: "ممر", hi: "मार्ग", ja: "通路")! Calibrated to genuine senses: passage of time / legislative passing (Vorbeigehen, passage, passagem, przejście, مرور, ผ่านไป), death/bereavement (Ableben, décès, decesso, falecimento, skon, zgon, aiziešana mūžībā, وفاة/رحيل, انتقال, 逝世), and fleeting/momentary adjective (vorübergehend, pasajero, éphémère, мимолетный, скороминущий).
7. Word 3373 (instructional): PART-OF-SPEECH BUG CAUGHT! Dump translated adjective 'instructional' as training/education nouns (fr: "entraînement", it: "formazione", kk: "білім беру"). Calibrated to genuine adjectives: lehrreich/pädagogisch, didáctico/instructivo, didactique/éducatif, dydaktyczny, formativo/didattico, öğretici, instrucional, تعليمي/تدريبي, آموزشی, 教学的/指导性的, оқыту/нұсқаулық.
8. Word 3383 (traditionally): PART-OF-SPEECH BUG CAUGHT! Dump used adjectives (ar: "تقليدي", hi: "पारंपरिक", ja: "伝統的な"). Calibrated to authentic adverbs: traditionell, tradicionalmente, traditionnellement, tradycyjnie, تقليدياً, पारंपरिक रूप से, 伝統的に, 传统上, дәстүрлі түрде.
9. Word 3385 (poorly): PART-OF-SPEECH & REGISTER BUG CAUGHT! Dump capitalized German "Schlecht", translated into French as "Gravement" (gravely), and used adjectives in Arabic ("سيئ"), Japanese ("悪い"), Kazakh ("жаман"). Calibrated to proper adverbs (schlecht/mangelhaft, mal/pobremente, mal/médiocrement, źle/słabo, male/scarsamente, kötü bir şekilde, بشكل سيئ, 不十分に/下手に, 糟糕地/贫乏地, нашар/жеткіліксіз) plus health state (kränklich, indispuesto, souffrant).
10. Word 3386 (conceive): SEMANTIC DRIFT CAUGHT! Dump limited 'conceive' only to biological pregnancy in Turkish ("hamile kalmak") or generic thinking in Kazakh/Japanese ("ойлау", "考える"). Calibrated across both core polysemous senses: conceiving an idea/plan (ersinnen/konzipieren, idear/imaginar, concevoir/imaginer, pojąć/wykoncypować, tasarlamak, يتصور/يبتكر, 构思/设想, 착상하다) and biological conception (empfangen, concebir/engendrar, tomber enceinte, począć, gebe kalmak, bear child, құрсақ көтеру).
11. Word 3388 (descend): REGISTER DRIFT CAUGHT! Dump translated 'descend' into German as overly generic "gehen" (to go!). Calibrated to precise directional movement (herabsteigen, sinken, bajar, descendre, scendere, aşağı inmek, түсу/төмендеу) and genealogical lineage (abstammen, descender de, descendre de, discendere da, soyundan gelmek, таралу).
12. Word 3352 (exploit): POLYSEMY HARMONIZATION! Harmonized the double senses of verb (to exploit workers / resources: ausbeuten, explotar, exploiter, wyzyskiwać, sfruttare, sömürmek, بهره‌برداری کردن, 剥削/利用) and heroic noun (feat/deed: Heldentat, hazaña, exploit, wyczyn, prodezza, kahramanlık, подвиг, ерлік).
13. Word 3364 (freak): COLLOQUIAL / FORMAL HARMONIZATION! Dump used purely clinical "abnormal" in German, Italian, Turkish, Arabic, and Kazakh. Calibrated to capture eccentric person, freak show, enthusiast/fanatic (Freak/Sonderling, bicho raro/friki, phénomène/maniaque, strambo/maniaco, ucube/acayip tip, 괴짜, 変人/フリーク, 怪人/狂热爱好者, оғаш адам).
14. Word 3370 (cease): PART-OF-SPEECH BUG CAUGHT! French dump had noun "arrêt". Calibrated to active verbs (aufhören/einstellen, cesar/detenerse, cesser/s'arrêter, ustać/zaprzestać, smettere/interrompere, durdurmak/sona ermek, يتوقف/يكف عن, やめる/終わる, 停止/终止, тоқтату/доғару).
15. Word 3375 (mentally): PART-OF-SPEECH BUG CAUGHT! Kazakh dump had noun "ақыл-ой" (mind). Calibrated to proper adverb: ақыл-ой жағынан, ойша, психикалық тұрғыдан.
16. Word 3381 (graphic): HARMONIZATION! Covered both vivid/explicit visual representation (anschaulich/bildhaft, ilustrativo/explícito, saisissant, obrazowy/dosadny, 생생한/사실적인, 生々しい) and computer/print graphic/chart (Grafik, gráfica, illustration, 图表, 图像, сызба).
17. Word 3392 (gathering): REGISTER FIX! Japanese dump had business conference "会議". Calibrated to social gathering / assembly (Versammlung/Zusammenkunft, reunión/encuentro, rassemblement/réunion, raduno/assemblea, toplantı/buluşma, 集まり/集会, 聚会/集会, жиын/басқосу).
18. Word 3400 (racist): NOUN/ADJECTIVE SYNTHESIS! German dump had noun only ("Rassist"), Japanese had noun only ("人種差別主義者"). Calibrated to cover both noun (racist person) and adjective (racist attitude/policy).
19. Brazilian Portuguese (pt / pt-BR) adaptations applied (travesseiro, bolsa, deslumbrante, presidiário, picape/caminhonete, febre, homólogo, etc.).
20. Latin American / Mexican Spanish (es) nuances applied (recluso/preso, bicho raro, didáctico, homólogo, fiebre, etc.).
21. Natural grammar in hi, ar, fa, ur, kk, bn rigorously verified.

====================================================
Audit Complete: 50 words calibrated across 35 target languages (36 locales total).
