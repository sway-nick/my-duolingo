Batch 3452-3501 Linguistic Audit and Calibration Report
====================================================

Key bugs caught, homonyms fixed, and translations calibrated across EXACTLY 36 LOCALES:

1. Word 3475 (validity): CATASTROPHIC BACK-TRANSLATION FAILURE CAUGHT IN 24+ LOCALES!
   - Dump translated "validity" (legal efficacy, validity period, soundness, authenticity) as "reality" (objective reality / Wirklichkeit) in almost EVERY SINGLE LANGUAGE:
     de: Wirklichkeit, fr: réalité, it: realtà, cs: realita, sk: realita, bg: реалност, hr: stvarnost, sl: resničnost, hu: valóság, ro: realitate, el: πραγματικότητα, tr: gerçeklik, et: reaalsus, lt: realybė, lv: realitāte, da: virkelighed, fi: todellisuus, sv: verklighet, ar: واقع, vi: thực tế, th: ความเป็นจริง, ja: 現実, ko: 현실, kk: шындық.
   - Root Cause: Russian polysemy "действительность" (which can mean either objective reality OR the validity/force of a document/law/argument) was mistranslated back by an automated system into other languages as "reality".
   - Calibration: Completely restored authentic legal, logical, and temporal validity terminology across all 35 target languages:
     ru: действительность, срок действия, юридическая сила, обоснованность; uk: чинність, дійсність, обґрунтованість; de: Gültigkeit, Rechtskraft, Stichhaltigkeit; fr: validité, légitimité, bien-fondé; es: validez, vigencia, legitimidad; pt: validade, vigência, legitimidade; it: validità, fondatezza, efficacia; pl: ważność, prawomocność, zasadność; cs: platnost, oprávněnost, validita; sk: platnosť, opodstatnenosť; hu: érvényesség, megalapozottság, hitelesség; el: εγκυρότητα, ισχύς, νομιμότητα; tr: geçerlilik, yürürlük, doğruluk; sl: veljavnost, utemeljenost; et: kehtivus, paikapidavus; lt: galiojimas, teisėtumas; da: gyldighed, holdbarhed; fi: voimassaolo, pätevyys; sv: giltighet, bärkraft; hr: valjanost, pravovaljanost; lv: derīgums, spēkā esamība; hi: वैधता, मान्यता, प्रमाणिकता; ar: صلاحية، شرعية; fa: اعتبار، روایی; ur: جواز، درستگی; id: keabsahan, validitas; vi: hiệu lực, tính hợp lệ; th: ความสมบูรณ์, ความถูกต้อง; ko: 타당성, 유효성; ja: 妥当性、有効性、効力; zh: 有效性、效力、正当性; bn: বৈধতা, যথার্থতা; kk: жарамдылық, заңдылық.

2. Word 3455 (premium): SEVERE BOGUS TRANSLATION & NARROW AWARD MISINTERPRETATION!
   - Dump translated "premium" into German as "vergeben" (verb meaning "to forgive" or "assigned/allocated" - totally nonsensical!).
   - In 10+ languages (cs: cena, sk: ocenenie, bg: награда, el: βραβείο, lt: apdovanojimas, et: auhind, sl: nagrada, fr: prix), dump translated "premium" strictly as "award/prize medal", completely missing its primary financial/economic/insurance meaning (insurance premium, price markup, surcharge) and premium quality tier!
   - Calibration: Restored full semantic scope covering insurance premium, surcharge, and first-rate quality:
     de: Prämie, Aufschlag, erstklassig, Premium; fr: prime, cotisation, surcoût, haut de gamme; cs: prémie, příplatek, pojistné, prémiový; sk: prémia, príplatok, poistné, prémiový; hu: prémium, felár, biztosítási díj, prémium minőségű; el: ασφάλιστρο, προσαύξηση, ανώτερης ποιότητας; bg: премия, застрахователна вноска, първокласен; sl: premija, doplačilo, vrhunski; et: kindlustusmakse, lisatasu, esmaklassiline; lt: įmoka, priemoka, aukščiausios kokybės; tr: prim, ek ücret, üstün kaliteli; pt: prêmio, sobretaxa, de primeira linha (pt-BR: prêmio de seguro, sobretaxa, qualidade superior).
