Batch 4102-4151 Linguistic Audit and Calibration Report
====================================================

Key linguistic calibrations and bugs caught across 36 locales:

1. Word 4102 ("dream of"):
   - Issue in dump: Russian had noun "мечта о" instead of verbal expression "мечтать о, грезить о".
   - French calibrated to "rêver de, songer à".
   - Spanish "soñar con", German "träumen von", Portuguese "sonhar com".
   - Non-Latin script calibration: natural verb collocations in Arabic (يحلم بـ، يطمح إلى), Urdu (کا خواب دیکھنا، حسرت کرنا), Hindi (का सपना देखना), Bengali (স্বপ্ন দেখা), Kazakh (армандау, қиялдау).

2. Word 4103 ("timber"):
   - Issue in dump: Mixed POS with adjective "древесина, лесной" and German "Bauholz, Holz", French "bois, forêt", Portuguese "madeira, floresta". "Timber" refers specifically to lumber/wood for building or harvested roundwood, not standing forest.
   - Calibrated across all locales: FR "bois de construction, bois d'œuvre", ES "madera, madera de construcción", PT "madeira, madeira de construção", PL "drewno budowlane, tarcica, drewno", TR "kereste, yapı kerestesi", IT "legname, legname da costruzione".

3. Word 4104 ("adore"):
   - Issue in dump: 1st person singular "обожаю" in Russian/Ukrainian ("обожнюю") instead of infinitive "обожать, очень любить, благоговеть".
   - Japanese had "崇拝する" (worship), calibrated to "熱愛する、大好きである".
   - All 35 target languages aligned to infinitive verb forms expressing deep affection and adoration.

4. Word 4105 ("realise"):
   - Polysemy addressed: covers both cognitive comprehension ("become aware / understand") and manifestation ("realize a project / achieve / fulfill").
   - Aligned RU "осознавать, понимать, осуществлять", UK "усвідомлювати, розуміти, здійснювати", DE "begreifen, einsehen, verwirklichen", ES "darse cuenta de, comprender, realizar", FR "se rendre compte, comprendre, réaliser".

5. Word 4106 ("I find it"):
   - Syntactic pattern calibration: English evaluation structure ("I find it difficult/useful" -> "мне это кажется / я считаю это").
   - Dump had literal "я нахожу это", Japanese "私はこれを見つける" (I locate this item!), Kazakh "мен оны таптым" (I found it!).
   - Fixed to evaluative predicates across all languages: JA "私はそれを〜だと思う、感じる", KO "나는 그것이 ~하다고 본다, 느낀다", ES "me parece, lo encuentro, lo considero", FR "je trouve cela, cela me paraît", KK "меніңше бұл, маған солай көрінеді", TR "bana ... geliyor, bunu ... buluyorum".

6. Word 4107 ("abolished"):
   - Part of speech: passive participle / adjective "упразднённый, отменённый, ликвидированный".
   - Fixed dump bugs: Urdu had transliterated gibberish "ابولیشڈ" (abolished), Hindi had bare "रद्द", German "abgeschafft, aufgehoben, beseitigt".
   - Calibrated legal/institutional terms: FR "aboli, supprimé, abrogé", ES "abolido, derogado, suprimido", IT "abolito, abrogato, soppresso", UR "منسوخ، ختم شدہ".

7. Word 4109 ("plug in"):
   - Phrasal verb with electrical preposition: "включать в розетку, подключать".
   - Dump had generic "connect" (FR "connecter", PT "conectar", HI "जुड़ना").
   - Calibrated to authentic electrical wall-socket idiom: FR "brancher, enficher, connecter", ES "enchufar, conectar a la corriente", PT "ligar à tomada, plugar", DE "einstecken, ans Netz anschließen", TR "fişe takmak, bağlamak".

8. Word 4110 ("be going to"):
   - Critical dump mistranslation: FR had imperative "Préparez-vous, planifiez" (Get ready, plan!), PT had imperative "Prepare-se, planeje", Kazakh had imperative "дайындал, жоспарла".
   - Fixed to prospective aspect / intentional future auxiliary: FR "aller, avoir l'intention de, s'apprêter à", PT "ir, pretender, estar prestes a", ES "ir a, tener la intención de", DE "vorhaben, beabsichtigen, werden", KK "істемекші болу, жиналу, ниеттену", JA "〜するつもりだ、〜しようとしている".

9. Word 4111 ("on time"):
   - Catastrophic dump mistranslation: FR had "pendant" (during!), PL had "podczas" (during!), IT had "durante" (during!). Machine translator confused "on time" with "at the time of / during the period of"!
   - Calibrated to punctuality and scheduled precision: FR "à l'heure, ponctuel, à temps", PL "na czas, punktualnie", IT "in orario, puntuale, in tempo", DE "pünktlich, rechtzeitig", ES "a tiempo, puntual, con puntualidad", PT "na hora certa, a tempo, pontualmente".

10. Word 4112 ("torch"):
    - Semantic homonymy / regional UK vs US: UK pocket torch (flashlight) vs classical burning stick (beacon/torch) vs slang/arson verb ("torch a building").
    - Calibrated across all locales: UK/US flashlight and flame distinction preserved: RU "фонарик, факел, поджигать", DE "Taschenlampe, Fackel, in Brand setzen", ES "linterna, antorcha, incendiar", FR "lampe de poche, torche, incendier", IT "torcia, lampadina tascabile, incendiare", PT "lanterna, tocha, incendiar".
