KEY SYSTEMIC BUGS CAUGHT & RECTIFIED:
------------------------------------
1. "Physically" Gloss Leak in Word 4153 ("look after"):
   The Russian source gloss "заботиться физически, присматривать" contained the explanatory word "физически" (meaning physical tending/care). Machine translation blindly translated "physically" across 15+ target languages:
   - RO: "a avea grijă fizic" -> rectified to "a avea grijă de, a supraveghea, a îngriji"
   - BG: "грижа се физически" -> rectified to "грижа се за, наглеждам, пазя"
   - HU: "fizikailag gondoskodni" -> rectified to "vigyázni rá, gondoskodni róla, ügyelni rá"
   - EL: "φροντίζω σωματικά" -> rectified to "φροντίζω, προσέχω, επιβλέπω"
   - SL: "skrbeti za fizično" -> rectified to "skrbeti za, paziti na, varovati"
   - ET: "füüsiliselt hoolitseda" -> rectified to "hoolitsema, järele vaatama, valvama"
   - LT: "rūpintis fiziškai" -> rectified to "prižiūrėti, rūpintis, saugoti"
   - DA: "omsorg fysisk" -> rectified to "passe på, tage sig af, holde øje med"
   - FI: "huolehtia fyysisesti" -> rectified to "pitää huolta, vahtia, huolehtia"
   - SV: "bry sig fysiskt" -> rectified to "se efter, passa, ta hand om"
   - HR: "fizički se brinuti" -> rectified to "paziti na, čuvati, brinuti se o"
   - LV: "rūpēties fiziski" -> rectified to "pieskatīt, uzraudzīt, rūpēties par"
   - AR: "يهتم جسدياً" -> rectified to "يعتني بـ، ينتبه إلى، يحرس"
   - TH: "ห่วงใยทางร่างกาย" -> rectified to "ดูแล, เฝ้าดู, เลี้ยงดู"
   - KK: "физикалық тұрғыдан қамқорлық жасау" -> rectified to "қарау, бағу, көз салып жүру"
   - CS/SK: "pečovat o sebe / starať sa o seba" (take care of ONESELF) -> completely inverted the meaning of transitive "look after"! Rectified to "starat se o, dohlížet na" / "starať sa o, dohliadať na".

2. Conjunction-for-Preposition Bug in Word 4167 ("annoyed about"):
   MT translated the preposition "about" as causal conjunction "because / because of that" across 14 languages:
   - DE: "verärgert, weil" -> rectified to "verärgert über etwas, genervt von etwas"
   - FR: "agacé parce que" -> rectified to "agacé par quelque chose, contrarié par quelque chose"
   - IT: "infastidito perché" -> rectified to "infastidito da qualcosa, irritato per qualcosa"
   - PT: "irritado porque" -> rectified to "chateado por causa de, irritado com algo"
   - RO: "enervat pentru că" -> rectified to "enervat din cauza a ceva, nemulțumit de ceva"
   - BG: "раздразнен, защото" -> rectified to "раздразнен от нещо, ядосан за нещо"
   - CS: "naštvaný, protože" -> rectified to "otrávený kvůli něčemu, naštvaný z něčeho"
   - SK: "naštvaný, pretože" -> rectified to "otrávený kvôli niečomu, nahnevaný z niečoho"
   - HU: "bosszantott, mert" -> rectified to "bosszús valami miatt, dühös valamiért"
   - EL: "ενοχλημένος επειδή" -> rectified to "εκνευρισμένος για κάτι, ενοχλημένος με κάτι"
   - SL: "jezen, ker" -> rectified to "razdražen zaradi nečesa, jezen zaradi nečesa"
   - ET: "pahane, sest" -> rectified to "ärritunud millegi peale, pahane millegi pärast"
   - LT: "susierzinęs, nes" -> rectified to "susierzinęs dėl ko nors, piktas dėl ko nors"
   - KK: "ашуланды, себебі" -> rectified to "бір нәрсе үшін ашулы, ренжулі"

3. Imperative Infection in Word 4158 ("think of"):
   Russian "вспомнить, придумывать" was misread by MT as imperatives ("вспомни, придумай"), producing command forms in 11 languages:
   - FR ("Souvenez-vous, inventez"), IT ("ricorda, inventa"), PT ("lembre-se, invente"), RO ("amintește-ți, inventează"), BG ("запомни, измисли"), SK ("pamätaj si, vymysli si"), HU ("emlékezz, találj ki"), EL ("θυμήσου, εφεύρεις"), SL ("zapomni si, izumi"), ET ("mäleta, leiuta"), LT ("prisimink, sugalvok").
   All restored to proper infinitive / base verbal forms.

4. Critical False Friend in Word 4179 ("maiden"):
   MT confused "maiden" (young unmarried woman; virgin; inaugural) with "maid" (housemaid, servant, domestic worker):
   - Hindi: "नौकरानी" (housemaid/servant) -> calibrated to "अविवाहित कन्या, युवती, प्रथम"
   - Japanese: "メイド" (cosplay maid / maidservant) -> calibrated to "乙女、少女、処女航海などの「初の」"
   - Arabic: "خادمة" (maidservant) -> calibrated to "عذراء، فتاة بكر، أول / تدشيني"
   - Kazakh: "пәк" (adjective virgin) -> calibrated to noun "бойжеткен, бикеш, алғашқы"
   - French: capitalized "Vierge" (Virgin Mary) -> calibrated to "jeune fille, donzelle, inaugural"

5. Hem of Garment Misinterpretation in Word 4173 ("edge"):
   Because Russian had "кромка", MT translated "edge" as garment hem/border across multiple languages:
   - DE ("Saum"), FR ("ourlet"), PT ("bainha"), HI ("हेम"), AR ("حاشية"), JA ("裾"), KK ("етек").
   Calibrated across all 35 languages to represent boundary, brink, cutting edge of blade, and competitive edge/advantage ("have an edge").

6. Live Animal vs. Prepared Meat in Word 4171 ("mutton"):
   "Mutton" specifically designates the meat of a mature sheep. MT translated it as live animal or lamb:
   - AR: "حمل" (young lamb animal) -> calibrated to "لحم الضأن، لحم الغنم"
   - JA: "子羊" (lamb animal) -> calibrated to "マトン、成羊の肉"
   - KK: "қозы" (young lamb) -> calibrated to "қой еті, ересек қойдың еті"
   - DE: "Hammel" (live wether) -> calibrated to "Hammelfleisch, Schaffleisch"
   - PT: "carneiro" (ram animal) -> calibrated to "carne de carneiro, carne de ovelha"

7. Truncated Senses in Word 4172 ("take off"):
   In JA, dump only had "値下げする" (reduce price)! In AR and KK, removing clothes was completely omitted.
   Calibrated to all three core semantic fields: remove clothing, aircraft departure/takeoff, and rapid rise to success.

8. Pragmatic Tone in Word 4154 ("look forward to"):
   VI had "chờ đợi thiếu kiên nhẫn" and TH had "รออย่างใจร้อน", conveying negative impatience or restlessness.
   Calibrated to positive, eager anticipation: "trông đợi, mong mỏi" in VI and "ตั้งตารอ, เฝ้ารอคอย" in TH.

9. Hostile vs. Neutral Calling in Word 4155 ("shout to"):
   "Shout to" is calling out to someone over a distance to get their attention, unlike "shout at" (scolding angrily).
   AR had "يصرخ في وجه شخص ما" (screaming in someone's face) and JA had "誰かに怒鳴る" (yelling angrily at someone).
   Rectified to calling out / hailing across distance.

10. Part of Speech Errors:
    - 4152 (care for): DA had noun "omsorg" -> rectified to verbal "drage omsorg for, passe på".
    - 4159 (short of): BG, EL, SL had 3rd person plural verbs ("изпитват недостиг", "βιώνουν έλλειψη", "doživijo pomanjkanje") -> rectified to adjectival phrases.
    - 4164 (safe from): TR had indicative verb "korunmaktadır" -> rectified to adjective phrase "...den güvende, korunmuş".
    - 4165 (free from): KK had noun "бостандық" (freedom) -> rectified to adjective "...-дан ада, құтылған, босатылған".
    - 4182 (fiscal cliff): Generic "economic crisis" in FR, PT, HI, AR, JA, KK -> calibrated to precise fiscal/budgetary cliff terms (falaise fiscale, abismo fiscal, 財政の崖, фискалдық құз).
    - 4183 (squat): FR had only "squatter" (illegal building occupation) -> expanded to crouching, squat exercises, and haunches.
    - 4187 (rusty): Expanded from purely corroded metal to figurative out-of-practice skills across all languages.
    - 4189 (melon): In AR, "بطيخ" commonly means watermelon; calibrated to "شمّام، بطيخ أصفر" for muskmelon/cantaloupe.
    - 4193 (barefoot): PT had "pés descalços" -> calibrated to adjective/adverb "descalço".
    - 4197 (I don't mind): Replaced broken fragments ("macht nichts aus" DE) with natural conversational expressions ("ich habe nichts dagegen, mir macht das nichts aus").
    - 4199-4201 (do the dishes, do laundry, do chores): Ensured natural domestic verb phrases (e.g. es-MX: lavar los trastes / lavar los platos; pt-BR: lavar a louça, lavar roupa, fazer as tarefas de casa).
