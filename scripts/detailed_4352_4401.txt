KEY BUGS CAUGHT, HOMONYMS FIXED, AND CALIBRATIONS APPLIED:
---------------------------------------------------------

1. Word 4374 (participle) - CATASTROPHIC SACRAMENT / GRAMMAR HOMONYM CONFUSION FIXED:
   - Dump translated Russian "причастие" (grammatical participle) through MT into religious sacrament / Holy Communion across multiple languages:
     - German dump had "Gemeinschaft" (church fellowship / communion) -> Fixed to "Partizip, Mittelwort".
     - French dump had "Communion" (Holy Communion) -> Fixed to grammatical "participe".
     - Ukrainian dump had "причастя" (Holy Communion) -> Fixed to correct Ukrainian linguistic term "дієприкметник".
     - Kazakh calibrated to standard linguistic term "есімше".
     - Hindi ("कृदंत"), Arabic ("اسم الفاعل أو المفعول"), Hungarian ("melléknévi igenév"), Polish ("imiesłów") all calibrated to authentic grammar terms.

2. Word 4362 (look out) - ADVERBIAL SEVERITY / WARNING FIX:
   - MT confused imperative exclamation "look out!" (warning of imminent danger) with "watchful / thorough / diligent":
     - German dump had "sorgfältig" (carefully / diligently!) -> Fixed to "Pass auf!, Achtung!, Vorsicht!".
     - French dump had "soigneusement" (neatly / carefully!) -> Fixed to "attention !, fais attention !, prends garde !".
     - Spanish calibrated with Latin American / Mexican alert: "¡cuidado!, ¡atención!, ¡aguas!".

3. Word 4391 (shoelace) - TEXTILE EMBROIDERY LACE BUG FIXED:
   - MT mistranslated "shoelace" as ornamental fabric lace (embroidery / crochet):
     - German dump had "Spitze" (textile lace / tip) -> Fixed to "Schnürsenkel, Schuhband, Schuhsenkel".
     - French dump had "dentelle" (needle lace) -> Fixed to "lacet, lacet de chaussure".
     - Spanish: calibrated to "cordón, cordón de zapato, agujeta" (including Mexican "agujeta").
     - Portuguese: calibrated to "cadarço, atacador" (distinguishing Brazilian "cadarço").

4. Word 4394 (saw) - DUAL HOMONYM DISAMBIGUATION (Saw Tool vs Past Tense of 'see'):
   - The dump contained conflicting translations across locales: some languages had only the cutting tool (piła, testere), while others had only the past tense of see (viděl, nägi, a vu).
   - All 35 target languages calibrated to comprehensively include both the primary intended tool/action ("пила, пилить; Säge, sägen; sierra, aserrar; scie, scier; testere, testereyle kesmek") and the past tense of see ("видел; sah; vio; a vu; gördü").

5. Word 4397 (look up to) - NOUN TO PHRASAL VERB CALIBRATION:
   - German dump had nouns "Respekt, Bewunderung" instead of active phrasal verbs.
   - Calibrated to German verbs: "aufblicken zu, bewundern, verehren".
   - Spanish: "admirar, respetar, tener como modelo".
   - Brazilian Portuguese: "admirar, respeitar, espelhar-se em, se inspirar em".

6. Word 4389 (harry up) - HEADWORD TYPO HANDLED & CALIBRATED:
   - Headword in dump had typo "harry up" for "hurry up" (transcription "ˈhɝi ʌp", Russian "поторопись").

7. Word 4352 (no way) - POLARITY REVERSAL RISK PREVENTED:
   - French dump had truncated string "certainem..." which risked meaning "certainement" (the exact polar opposite of "no way!").
