2. Word 4565 (debt): MASSIVE 12-LANGUAGE "MORAL DUTY" & "CUSTOMS TARIFF" FALSE FRIEND!
   - Dump translated financial "debt" (Russian "долг") into moral duty / moral obligation (de: Pflicht, fr: devoir, it: dovere, pt: obrigação, cs: povinnost, sk: povinnosť, sl: dolžnost, et: kohustus, lt: pareiga) and customs tariff / import duty (bg: мито, hu: vám, el: δασμός)!
   - Root Cause: Russian "долг" polysemy ("денежный долг" = monetary debt vs "гражданский долг" = civic duty). Automated translation latched onto "duty", translating it as moral duty in 9 languages and customs duty in 3 languages.
   - Calibration: Restored precise financial debt terminology across all 35 target languages:
     de: Schulden, Schuld, Verbindlichkeit; fr: dette, créance; it: debito, pendenza; pt: dívida, débito; cs: dluh, pohledávka; sk: dlh, zadlženosť; sl: dolg, zadolženost; et: võlg, võlgnevus; lt: skola, įsiskolinimas; bg: дълг, задължение; hu: adósság, tartozás; el: χρέος, οφειλή.

3. Word 4592 (purpose): ARCHERY / SHOOTING TARGET / BULLSEYE SKEW!
   - Dump translated "purpose" (Russian "цель") as a physical shooting target or bullseye:
     it: "bersaglio" (target/bullseye!)
     fr: "cible" (target!)
     ro: "ţintă" (target!)
     sl: "tarča" (target!)
     et: "sihtmärk" (target!)
     lt: "taikinys" (target!)
   - Root Cause: Russian "цель" can be an archery target or an abstract aim/purpose. In English, "purpose" is exclusively aim, intention, raison d'être, or function.
   - Calibration: Calibrated to aim, intention, objective, and purpose:
     it: scopo, proposito, finalità; fr: but, dessein, finalité; ro: scop, menire, intenție; sl: namen, smisel, cilj; et: eesmärk, siht, kavatsus; lt: tikslas, paskirtis, ketinimas.

4. Word 4600 (possibility): "OPPORTUNITY" SYSTEMIC OVERRIDE!
   - Dump translated "possibility" (Russian "возможность") as "opportunity" (a favorable occasion / chance) across 15+ languages:
     de: Gelegenheit; fr: opportunité; it: opportunità; ro: oportunitate; cs: příležitost; sk: príležitosť; el: ευκαιρία; sl: priložnost; hr: prilika; ar: الفرصة; id: Peluang; vi: Cơ hội; th: โอกาส; ko: 기회; ja: 機会.
   - Root Cause: Russian "возможность" ambiguously spans both possibility (possibilitas) and opportunity (opportunitas). The English headword is strictly "possibility" (potential, likelihood, option).
   - Calibration: Calibrated to possibility, likelihood, and contingency:
     de: Möglichkeit, Wahrscheinlichkeit; fr: possibilité, éventualité; it: possibilità, eventualità; ro: posibilitate, eventualitate; cs: možnost, pravděpodobnost; sk: možnosť, pravdepodobnosť; el: πιθανότητα, δυνατότητα; sl: možnost, verjetnost; hr: mogućnost, vjerojatnost; ar: إمكانية, احتمال; id: kemungkinan, kebolehjadian; vi: khả năng, sự có thể; th: ความเป็นไปได้; ko: 가능성, 가망; ja: 可能性, 見込み.
