Batch 4702-4751 Linguistic Audit and Calibration Report
====================================================

Key bugs caught, homonyms fixed, and translations calibrated across EXACTLY 36 LOCALES:

1. Word 4746 (least): CATASTROPHIC OPPOSITE MEANING BUG IN KOREAN!
   - Dump translated "least" into Korean as "무엇보다도" (which means "above all", "more than anything" — the direct polar opposite of "least"!).
   - Root Cause: MT engine confused "least" with "at least / in the least / not in the least" and inverted polarity to "above all".
   - Calibration: Restored authentic superlative terms meaning lowest/least degree:
     ko: 가장 적은, 최소한, 가장 적게.

2. Word 4705 (movie): PHOTOGRAPHIC CELLULOID FILM IN KO & JA!
   - Dump translated "movie" into Korean as "필름" (celluloid roll of film) and Japanese as "フィルム" (camera/cinematographic film roll).
   - In modern natural Korean and Japanese, a motion picture / film / movie is "영화" (ko) and "映画" (ja).
   - Calibration:
     ko: 영화; ja: 映画.

3. Word 4749 (maybe): SYSTEMIC PART-OF-SPEECH COLLAPSE ("CAN BE" VERBS / SENTENCE SUFFIXES)!
   - Automated translation systems translated "maybe" as literal verbal phrases ("can be") or dependent clause endings rather than standalone adverbs:
     - Romanian: "Pot fi" ("they can be" — 3rd person plural verb!) -> Calibrated to: poate, posibil, s-ar putea.
     - Czech: "Může být" (literal verbal phrase "can be") -> Calibrated to: možná, snad, třeba.
     - Slovak: "Môže byť" (literal verbal phrase "can be") -> Calibrated to: možno, snáď, azda.
     - Danish: "kan være" (literal verb phrase "can be") -> Calibrated to: måske, eventuelt, vel sagtens.
     - Finnish: "saattaa olla" (literal verb phrase "may be") -> Calibrated to: ehkä, kenties, mahdollisesti.
     - Swedish: "kan vara" (literal verb phrase "can be") -> Calibrated to: kanske, möjligen.
     - Greek: "Μπορεί να είναι" (verbal phrase "it may be") -> Calibrated to: ίσως, μπορεί.
     - Latvian: "varbūt ir" (literal "maybe is") -> Calibrated to: varbūt, iespējams.
     - Arabic: "قد يكون" (verbal clause "it may be") -> Calibrated to: ربما، قد يكون، لعل.
     - Korean: "~일 수도 있다" (dependent sentence ending suffix!) -> Calibrated to standalone adverb: 아마도, 어쩌면.
     - Japanese: "～かもしれない" (dependent sentence ending grammar point!) -> Calibrated to standalone adverb: たぶん, おそらく.
     - French: "Peut être" (spelled with space as two words instead of standard hyphenated adverb "peut-être").

4. Word 4748 (loudly): SYSTEMIC ADJECTIVE INFECTION ACROSS 7+ LOCALES!
   - Dump translated adverb "loudly" using nominative adjectives:
     - Polish: "głośny" (masculine adjective) -> Calibrated to adverb: głośno, donośnie, hałaśliwie.
     - Czech: "hlasitý" (adjective) -> Calibrated to adverb: nahlas, hlasitě.
     - Slovak: "hlasný" (adjective) -> Calibrated to adverb: nahlas, hlasno.
     - Hungarian: "hangos" (adjective) -> Calibrated to adverb: hangosan, lármásan.
     - Greek: "μεγαλόφωνος" (adjective) -> Calibrated to adverb: δυνατά, μεγαλόφωνα.
     - Estonian: "vali" (adjective) -> Calibrated to adverb: valjusti, valjult, kõva häälega.
     - Bulgarian: "силен" (adjective) -> Calibrated to adverb: силно, гръмко, на висок глас.

5. Word 4707 (performance): MECHANICAL EFFICIENCY / WORK PRODUCTIVITY FALSE FRIEND BUG!

     - Polish: "wydajność" (throughput, efficiency of a machine!) -> Calibrated to: występ, przedstawienie, wykonanie.
     - Czech: "výkon" (power output!) -> Calibrated to: představení, vystoupení, výkon.
     - Slovak: "výkon" -> Calibrated to: predstavenie, vystúpenie, výkon.
