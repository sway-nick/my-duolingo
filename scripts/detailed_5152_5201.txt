1. Word 5154 (mold): POLYSEMY & DUAL CONFLATION BUG!
   - Dump conflated fungal growth (mold/mildew) with container/matrix used to cast shapes (mold/mould).
   - In French, the dump provided only "moule" (baking pan/matrix/mussel), omitting "moisissure" (fungus).
   - In Hungarian, the dump provided only "forma" (casting mold), omitting "penész" (fungus).
   - In Greek, the dump provided only "καλούπι" (casting mold), omitting "μούχλα" (fungus).
   - In Russian, the dump had only "плесень" (fungus), missing the industrial/craft sense of molding.
   - Fixed: Calibrated across all 35 target languages to comprehensively encompass both fungal organism and industrial shaping matrix/verb (ru: "плесень; форма, лекало; формовать", fr: "moisissure; moule; façonner", de: "Schimmel; Gussform; formen", es: "moho; molde; moldear", pt: "mofo, bolor; molde; moldar").

   - Root Cause: MT parsed "fastener" as an active imperative/infinitive verb of grasping instead of a physical fastener hardware/device (clasps, zippers, snaps, bolts, rivets).
   - Fixed: Restored noun status across all languages (ru: "застежка, фиксатор, крепежная деталь", de: "Verschluss, Schließe, Befestigungselement", pt: "fecho, prendedor, presilha", vi: "chốt khóa, móc gài, khóa cài", ja: "留め具、ファスナー、止め金具", ko: "조임쇠, 잠금장치, 결속구", kk: "бекіткіш, түймелегіш, қапсырма").
