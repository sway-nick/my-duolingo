1. Word 5302 (concealment):
   - Machine translation confused "concealment" (hiding, disguising oneself or assets, shielding) with costume disguise in several languages:
     es: "disfraz" (fancy dress/costume) -> calibrated to "ocultamiento, encubrimiento, camuflaje".
     fr: "cachotterie" (petty childish secrecy) -> calibrated to "dissimulation, recel, camouflage".
     tr: "kılık değiştirme" (dressing up in disguise) -> calibrated to "gizleme, saklama, kamufle etme".
     da: "forklædning", sv: "förklädnad", el: "μεταμφίεση", sl: "preobleka" -> fixed from costume disguise to concealment/secrecy.
     de: fixed lowercase verb "verbergen" to capitalized nouns: "Verheimlichung, Verbergen, Tarnung".

2. Word 5303 (concession):
   - Incomplete single-sense translations expanded across all languages to include both political/argumentative concession (yielding/compromise) and commercial/governmental concession (charter/franchise/licence).

3. Word 5304 (concord) & 5305 (concurrence):

5. Word 5308 (confederation):
