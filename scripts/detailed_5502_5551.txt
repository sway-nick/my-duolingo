Key bugs caught, homonyms fixed, and translations calibrated across EXACTLY 36 LOCALES:

1. Word 5514 (amortize): CATASTROPHIC PILLOW / CUSHION / SHOCK-ABSORBER MISTRANSLATION ACROSS 22 LANGUAGES!
   - Dump translated financial verb "amortize" (write off debt/asset over time) as literal cushion/pillow/padding or mechanical shock absorber:
     de: "polstern" (to pad/cushion a chair!)
     es: "amortiguar" (cushion / shock absorb!)
     it: "attutire" (dampen/muffle!)
     tr: "yastıklamak" (to cushion with a pillow!)
     pt: "amortecer" (soften/cushion!)
     bg: "възстановявам" (restore?!)
     cs: "zmírnit" (mitigate!)
     sk: "zmierniť" (mitigate!)
     hu: "kipárnáz" (pad with cushions!)
     el: "προστατεύω" (protect!)
     sl: "ublažiti" (mitigate!)
     et: "pehmendama" (soften!)
     lt: "pagalvėlė" (literally "little pillow / cushion" - noun!)
     da: "dæmpe" (dampen!)
     fi: "pehmentää" (soften!)
     sv: "dämpa" (dampen!)
     hr: "ublažiti" (mitigate!)
     lv: "spilvens" (literally "pillow" - noun!)
     ar: "حماية" (protection!)
     vi: "đệm" (mattress/cushion!)
     th: "รองรับ" (support/cushion!)
     ko: "완충하다" (buffer/cushion!)
     ja: "緩衝する" (cushion/buffer!)
     kk: "жастық" (literally "pillow" - noun!)
   - Root Cause: Confusion between financial amortization and the Spanish/Portuguese false friend "amortiguar / amortecer" (shock absorber) and Russian "амортизатор", culminating in Baltic/Slavic/Turkic translating it as literal pillow/cushion!
   - Calibrated: Corrected all languages to financial loan repayment, depreciation, debt payoff, write-off:
     de: "amortisieren, tilgen, abschreiben", es: "amortizar, liquidar una deuda", fr: "amortir, rembourser graduellement",
     pl: "amortyzować, spłacać w ratach", it: "ammortizzare, estinguere gradualmente", tr: "amorti etmek, itfa etmek",
     pt: "amortizar, abater dívida", lt: "amortizuoti, išmokėti dalimis", lv: "amortizēt, atmaksāt pa daļām",
     kk: "борышты өтеу, амортизациялау", vi: "khấu hao, trả góp dần", th: "ผ่อนชำระหนี้สิน, ตัดจำหน่าย".
