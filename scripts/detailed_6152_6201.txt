     * et: "vabanema kuritarvitamisest"
     * lt: "atsikratyti piktnaudžiavimo"
     * da: "slippe af med misbrug"
     * fi: "päästä eroon väärinkäytöksistä"

   - Dump translated "demur" (verb: to balk, hesitate, make objection) by looking up synonym "object" and picking physical object/thing/item:
     * it: "oggetto" (physical item!)
     * ro: "obiect" (physical object!)
     * hu: "tárgy" (physical object!)
     * el: "αντικείμενο" (physical object!)
     * et: "objekt" (physical object!)
     * lt: "objektas" (physical object!)
     * lv: "objekts" (physical object!)
     * hr: "predmet" (physical object!)
     * da: "genstand" (physical object!)
     * fi: "esine" (physical object!)
     * sv: "objekt" (physical object!)
     * vi: "đối tượng" (target / object!)
     * ko: "대상" (target / object!)
     * kk: "нысана" (target / mark!)
   - Calibrated across all locales to genuine objection/hesitation verbs:
     ru: "возражать, колебаться; возражение", it: "obiettare, fare obiezioni, esitare", fi: "vastustaa, epäröidä", ja: "異議を唱える、難色を示す", etc.

   - Dump translated "dross" (slag, waste, rubbish, impurity) as "to refuse / reject":

     * ko: "거부하다" (to refuse verb!)
     * lv: "atteikt" (to refuse verb!)

     * sv: "vägra" (to refuse verb!)
     * vi: "từ chối" (to refuse verb!)

   - Calibrated to authentic terms for scum, dross, slag, waste:

5. Word 6183 (disparate):
   - Conflation of "disparate" (fundamentally unlike, incompatible) with "incomparable = matchless / peerless" across 16 languages!
   - Dump translated disparate as peerless / matchless excellence:
     * ja: "比類のない" (peerless, unrivaled!)
     * fi: "vertaansa vailla oleva" (matchless, unrivaled!)
     * da: "uforlignelig" (peerless!)
     * sv: "ojämförlig" (peerless!)
     * th: "หาที่เปรียบไม่ได้" (matchless!)
     * ko: "비교할 수 없는" (incomparable!)
   - Calibrated to correct meaning (fundamentally different, heterogeneous, clashing):
     ja: "異種の、まったく異なる、共通点のない", fi: "täysin erilainen, yhteismitaton", sv: "väsensskild, helt olikartad", etc.

     * es: "disidente"
     * it: "dissenziente"
     * ro: "disident"
     * ru: "несогласный"
     * ja: "反対する"
   - Calibrated to musical / aesthetic / conceptual dissonance and discord:
     ru: "несозвучный, диссонирующий, противоречивый", ja: "不協和の、耳障りな、一致しない", vi: "nghịch tai, bất hòa, chói tai", etc.

   - Calibrated to impartial, selfless, objective:
     ru: "бескорыстный, беспристрастный, нейтральный", de: "uneigennützig, unparteiisch, sachlich", fi: "puolueeton, pyyteetön", etc.

9. Word 6184 (dissemble):

   - Calibrated across all locales.
