1. Word 6353 (implode):

     The raw dump translated 'implode' as 'взрываться' (explode outward) in Russian,
     'explodieren' in German, and 'exploser' in French. Implosion is violent inward collapse!
   - Calibrated to inward collapse / inward bursting across all locales:
     * ru: 'схлопываться, разрушаться внутрь, взрываться внутрь'
     * de: 'implodieren, nach innen zusammenstürzen'
     * fr: 'imploser, s'effondrer vers l'intérieur'
     * ja: '内破する、内部へ崩壊する'

2. Word 6354 (importune):
   - PART OF SPEECH CONFUSION IN RAW DUMP:

     * es: 'importunar, acosar con ruegos, atosigar'

3. Word 6363 (incandescent):
   - TRUNCATION / NOUN SUBSTITUTION BUG IN RAW DUMP:

     * fr: 'incandescent, éclatant, ardent'
     * ja: '白熱した、白熱光を発する、情熱的な'

4. Word 6374 (incumbent):
   - OVERLY NARROW DEFINITION IN RAW DUMP:
     Raw dump translated 'incumbent' as 'действующий президент' (active president).

     * de: 'amtierend, Amtsinhaber, obliegend'
     * es: 'titular, en ejercicio, ocupante del cargo'

5. Word 6387 (infamous):

6. Word 6389 (infer):

7. Word 6394 (ingenuous):
